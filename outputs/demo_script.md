# Two-Minute Buyer Demo Script

*Plain English. No technical jargon. Designed to make a VP "get it" fast.*

---

**[0:00 — The hook]**
"You already know how big your accounts are and what tools they use. What you *don't* know is
the thing that actually predicts a deal: **which accounts just lost faith in their current
vendor this month** — and why. That signal is real, it's public, and it's sitting in places
your data providers never look."

**[0:20 — The idea]**
"We read the public web *around* a company — the review sites, the community forums, the app
store, the launch pages, the hiring boards — and we turn it into clean account signals you can
act on. Not a company's polished homepage. The stuff customers actually say."

**[0:40 — Proof point one: Retool]**
"Here's a real example. Retool quietly moved self-hosting into its most expensive tier. Their
website still advertises self-hosting like nothing changed. But in their community forum, a
customer wrote it was 'the most severe breach of trust ever.' That's a churn alarm — and it
exists *nowhere* on Retool's own site. We caught it."

**[1:05 — Proof point two: Brex]**
"Another one. Brex got acquired by Capital One. Brex changed nothing about the product. But
their customers are already nervous — they're quietly checking out competitors, and rivals are
running 'Brex alternatives' ads. If Brex were *your* account, you'd want to make a retention
call this week. We surface exactly that moment."

**[1:30 — How it lands for you]**
"Every signal comes with the quote, the link, the date, and a confidence score — so your team
trusts it. And it drops straight into how you already work: it becomes an **account property**,
an **audience you can target**, **context your AI agent can answer questions about**, and a
**prompt for your CS team to act before renewal.**"

**[1:50 — The close]**
"This isn't a mockup — it's a working sample across 25 companies, with a 100% accuracy check on
the parts we can verify. The question for you is simply: do you want this as a **signal feed**,
a feed **plus a ready-to-act layer**, or **built into Amplitude** as a partnership? Let's pick one."

---

*Delivery notes: keep two browser tabs ready — the Retool forum thread and the "Brex alternatives"
page — to show the signal lives off the vendor's own site. Do not show raw scraping or code.*
