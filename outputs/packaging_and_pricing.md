# Packaging & Pricing — External Account Signal Graph

*Three commercial packages, in increasing depth and commitment. Pricing is indicative
positioning, not a quote — designed to anchor a conversation with Amplitude.*

---

## Package 1 — Signal Feed
**"Know which accounts are in motion."**

- **What's included:** the Account + Account-Signals + Derived-State layers (Schema layers 1–3)
  for your target account list. Refreshed feed of evidence-backed signals — trust events,
  pricing backlash, acquisition uncertainty, competitor decline, hiring/launch momentum — each
  with source URL, evidence quote, confidence, and date.
- **Delivery:** scheduled export (CSV/JSON) + a synced **Amplitude account-property** push.
- **Who buys it:** VP Product / VP CS who want early-warning and competitive context.
- **Why it's safe:** safe-public + licensed sources only; no contact data.
- **Indicative pricing:** **annual subscription, tiered by # of tracked accounts** (e.g. banded at
  500 / 2,000 / 10,000 accounts). Lowest commitment, fastest to value.

## Package 2 — Signal Feed + Activation Layer
**"Know, *and* act — in the tools you already use."**

- **Everything in Package 1, plus:**
  - **Recommended-Action layer** (Schema layer 4): account property, audience trigger, AI-Agent
    context, and CS/expansion play generated per signal.
  - **Optional, permission-gated Activation/Contact layer** (Schema layer 5) via **licensed**
    providers (ZoomInfo/Apollo) — role-based routing, legal-basis required, suppression honored.
  - Pre-built **Amplitude audiences** and CS playbook templates.
- **Who buys it:** VP CS / VP Growth who want signals to drive retention and expansion motions.
- **Why it's safe:** activation layer is off by default and only ever connects to *licensed*
  contact sources — never scraped. Compliant with GDPR/CCPA/CAN-SPAM by configuration.
- **Indicative pricing:** **subscription + activation add-on** (per-seat for the playbook UI, or a
  success fee tied to influenced retention/expansion).

## Package 3 — Embedded API / Strategic Partnership
**"Make this a native Amplitude capability."**

- **What's included:** the signal graph delivered as an **embedded API** behind Amplitude's own
  product (account properties, audiences, and **AI-Agent context** powered natively), co-built
  roadmap, and a **licensed-source procurement plan** so Amplitude ships it enterprise-safe.
- **Who buys it:** VP Product / VP AI / VP Partnerships — this is a build-vs-partner decision.
- **Commercials:** **platform/partnership deal** — annual platform fee + revenue share or OEM
  licensing; joint data-rights and compliance ownership.
- **Why it's the endgame:** the signal becomes a defensible feature *inside* Amplitude (CS,
  audiences, Agents), not a side feed — and the differentiator (off-site customer voice) is
  hardest for incumbents to copy.

---

## Recommendation
**Lead with Package 1 to prove value fast, with a clear upgrade path to Package 2**, and position
**Package 3 as the strategic prize.** Land on the Signal Feed (low risk, evidence-backed, quick
integration), expand into Activation once CS sees retention lift, and use that proof to justify
an embedded partnership. Gate every tier on the source-tier discipline in
`outputs/risk_and_production_path.md` — risky sources never enter a paid feed un-licensed.
