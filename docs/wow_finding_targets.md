# Wow-Finding Targets — Flagship Narrative Candidates

**Product:** External Product Intelligence Graph · **Buyer:** Amplitude · **Date:** 2026-05-21

The goal of a "wow finding": a **specific, defensible, multi-source story** that makes a VP stop and pay attention — *Company X removed Pendo from its integrations page in March, started hiring three product analysts mentioning Amplitude in April, and its last 30 G2 reviews show competitor-displacement language.* Each candidate below is a **hypothesis to confirm in Phase 1**, but every one is anchored to **at least one real, observed, cited data point** today. Ranked by likely impact.

> Honesty note: these are *narrative hypotheses*, not yet confirmed multi-source findings. The "preliminary evidence" is real and cited; the leap to a *named company telling the full three-leg story* is the Phase-1 job. No fabricated specifics.

## Rank 1 (highest) — Heap-customer displacement post-Contentsquare

- **Hypothesized narrative:** A company running **Heap** is migrating off it after the Contentsquare acquisition (roadmap/pricing uncertainty), toward Hotjar / FullStory / PostHog / **Amplitude** — a clean "pain-emergence + competitor-displacement" story.
- **Source types needed to confirm:** (a) Heap removed from the company's integrations/docs page; (b) a job post referencing "migrating from Heap" or standing up a new analytics vendor; (c) Reddit/G2 language about Heap pricing/roadmap pain post-acquisition.
- **Preliminary evidence (real, cited):** Heap acquired by Contentsquare, completed 2023-12-07 ([Contentsquare press](https://contentsquare.com/press/contentsquare-completes-acquisition-heap/)). Population-level net-negative migration is documented: ~3,019 companies left Heap for Hotjar vs 997 inbound (~3:1); ~2.2:1 loss vs FullStory ([OpenPanel](https://openpanel.dev/compare/heap-alternative), [TechnologyChecker](https://technologychecker.io/technology/heap)).
- **Why it wows Amplitude:** It's a live displacement market where Amplitude is a destination. A named, evidenced switcher is a direct expansion/win target.

## Rank 2 — Mixpanel pricing-pain switcher

- **Hypothesized narrative:** A growth-stage SaaS on **Mixpanel** hits unpredictable event-based cost ceilings, is hiring a product/data analyst, and is evaluating **Amplitude** or PostHog.
- **Source types needed:** (a) JD mentions Mixpanel + "evaluating/migrating"; (b) an open product/data-analyst role; (c) G2/Reddit pricing-pain language.
- **Preliminary evidence (real, cited):** Documented Mixpanel pricing pain — users capping event tracking to stay in budget; Growth-plan features behind paid add-ons; Amplitude launched a $49/mo Plus plan explicitly to counter such complaints ([Userpilot](https://userpilot.com/blog/mixpanel-vs-amplitude/)).
- **Why it wows Amplitude:** Pricing-driven displacement directly into Amplitude's funnel; the external pain signal *is* the buying signal.

## Rank 3 — PostHog consolidation (displacement risk *away from* Amplitude)

- **Hypothesized narrative:** A company is consolidating onto **PostHog** (analytics + flags + experiments) and away from Amplitude/Mixpanel — signaled by docs/integration changes plus analytics-engineering hires.
- **Source types needed:** (a) PostHog references replacing a prior vendor in docs/integrations; (b) "PostHog" in JDs; (c) Reddit migration threads.
- **Preliminary evidence (real, cited):** PostHog ships **managed migrations** that auto-export from Amplitude and Mixpanel into its schema — a productized, frictionless switching path ([PostHog migrate-from-Amplitude](https://posthog.com/docs/migrate/migrate-from-amplitude)).
- **Why it wows Amplitude:** This is the *defensive* wow — Accel can flag Amplitude's own at-risk accounts (churn-prevention), not just expansion targets. A VP feels this one personally.

## Rank 4 — Brex post-Capital One acquisition: stack rationalization

- **Hypothesized narrative:** Brex's **$5.15B acquisition by Capital One (completed 2026-04-07)** triggers tooling consolidation and reorg; displacement/pain signals likely surface in reviews and restructuring-era hiring.
- **Source types needed:** (a) integration/tooling changes post-close; (b) hiring pattern (freeze vs targeted analyst hires); (c) Reddit/Glassdoor transition sentiment.
- **Preliminary evidence (real, cited):** Acquisition announced 2026-01-22, completed 2026-04-07 ([Capital One newsroom](https://www.capitalone.com/about/newsroom/capital-one-completes-acquisition-of-brex/)).
- **Why it wows Amplitude:** M&A is a top expansion-intelligence trigger; the org-change leg is already primary-sourced.

## Rank 5 — Intercom→Fin rebrand: competitive displacement vs Zendesk

- **Hypothesized narrative:** Intercom's **2026-05-12 rebrand to "Fin"** with an AI-native rebuilt helpdesk is an aggressive displacement play against Zendesk; the inverse story — customers churning during the rebrand/repricing ($0.99/resolution model) — is the angle to validate.
- **Source types needed:** (a) repositioning/removal of legacy "messenger"; (b) hiring around Fin/AI-agent roles; (c) G2/Reddit resolution-pricing pain or Zendesk migration language.
- **Preliminary evidence (real, cited):** Rebrand and "Intercom 2" rebuild documented ([StackSwap](https://stackswap.ai/intercom-becomes-fin)); Fin $0.99/resolution vs Zendesk per-agent framing ([HelpCrunch](https://helpcrunch.com/blog/zendesk-vs-intercom/)). *(Rebrand date is secondary-source — moderate confidence; confirm directly in Phase 1.)*
- **Why it wows Amplitude:** A pricing-model disruption with two-sided displacement; rich review-language signal once collected.

**Selection guidance for the flagship (Phase 10):** prioritize a candidate where all three legs can be confirmed *and* the company is a verifiable Amplitude customer or clean expansion target. Rank 1 (Heap displacement) and Rank 3 (PostHog defensive) are the strongest for an Amplitude audience because Amplitude is a named party in each.
